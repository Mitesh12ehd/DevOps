Job control:
    - to manage multiple jobs from single terminal
    - we can 
        - start command in background
        - suspend running foreground process 
        - Resume suspended commands in the foreground.
        - Resume suspended commands in the background.
        - List active and stopped jobs 
        - terminate jobs
    - it manage job associated with current shell.  

    - check job control is enabled or not
        set -o | grep monitor
    - enable
        set -m
    - disable
        set +m

Job vs process:
    - process is individual program running 
    - A job is a command or pipeline managed by the shell.

    ex. 
        sleep 100 | cat &
        [1] 2456

        - this is one background job, it can involve multiple processes.
        - 1: Job Id
        - 2456: Process id

Listing jobs
    - jobs

    ex. 
        sleep 100 &
        sleep 200 &
        sleep 300 &
        jobs

    o/p:
        [1]   Running    sleep 100 &
        [2]-  Running    sleep 200 &
        [3]+  Running    sleep 300 &

    + current job
    - previous job
    no symbol: other job

Reference jobs:
    %1	Job number 1
    %2	Job number 2
    %+	Current job
    %%	Current job
    %-	Previous job
    %string	    Job whose command begins with string
    %?string	Job whose command contains string

    ex. 
        sleep 100 &
        sleep 200 &

        # refer first job 
        jobs %1
        jobs %+

Moving a Foreground Process to the Background:
    - if we have write 
        sleep 100
    - we want to move to background 
        1. ctrl + z : suspend the foreground job
        2. verify, job
            [1]+  Stopped    sleep 100
        2. Resuming suspended job in background
            bg
            # o/p: [1]+ sleep 100 &

    - specify job 
        bg %1

Bringing a Background Job to the Foreground – fg
    - sleep 100 &
    - fg

    - specify process 
        fg %1

disown – Removing a Job from Shell Management
    sleep 300 &
    disown %1
